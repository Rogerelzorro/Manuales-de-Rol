Hello! Thank you so much for purchasing One Shots: American Dreams!

The main content is in the PDF file entitled "One Shots - American Dreams.pdf"

Each "Materials" folder contains:
* character sheets for each one-shot, prefilled and ready to be printed. 
* A black-and-white booklet. You will notice when you open them that they are formatted vertically and the pagination is odd. This is because the booklets are designed to be printed, folded down the middle, and stapled, like a zine. This is to print it on the smallest amount of paper possible.
